THE STORY ARC (Memorize this—it's your backbone)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. PROBLEM: Off-hand control feels blind (elderly, surgeons, drone pilots)
2. INSIGHT: Your brain extends into tools. Needs feedback to learn.
3. QUESTION: Does haptic, audio, or nothing work best?
4. DESIGN: Game + 25 people + 3 feedback types + 4 difficulty levels
5. DATA: No modality difference. Everyone follows same pattern.
6. PLOT TWIST: Baseline was 10× different BEFORE any feedback!
7. LESSON: Task difficulty dominates. Modality doesn't predict outcomes.
8. REAL INSIGHT: Individual baseline matters more than which technology.
9. IMPLICATION: Don't pick tech and hope. Measure baseline FIRST.
10. CLOSE: This study said "No" to modality. But "Yes" to individual differences.
