## **SLIDE 6**

**What to Say:**
"Both graphs show the same story: all three groups look identical. Kruskal-Wallis p > 0.19. Bayes factors (BF₀₁ ≈ 84) show strong evidence for NO effect. Effect sizes negligible (r = ±0.25). Sample sizes n = 8–9 per group."

**Transition:**
"But wait—something surprising happened before we even started testing..."

---

## **SLIDE 7**

**What to Say:**
"Audio baseline: 0.33 falls. Haptic baseline: 4.88 falls. That's 10× difference. Groups weren't equal from the start (Mann-Whitney U = 28, p = 0.04). Right panel shows: all groups respond to warnings similarly when we look at proximity recovery and tilt correction."

**Transition:**
"So what does that mean for learning over time?"

## **SLIDE 8**
